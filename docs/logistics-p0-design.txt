SHINOBI HOLLOW — LOGISTICS / SAFARI P0 DESIGN
Scope: diagnostics, bounded sprite memory, Porter House, physical shelves,
conserved goods, pickup/delivery claims and visible shortages. Stop after P0.

Evidence and disagreements
The imported v19.1 already sells delivered shopGoods/shopWares, with 10 raw
and 10 crafted items per shop, plus partial incoming-haul reservations.
The brief's pooled-shop baseline describes the older repository import.
Baseline the current v19.1 economy before any behavior changes. Its partial
256-entry rig eviction misses the left-facing insertion branch; use one
1000-entry global LRU on every insertion. Do not change demand, prices,
building production rates, service limits or contracts before P1 approval.

Goods and claims
Keep G.stock/G.wares as central storage and migrate local stock into b.shelf
with at least the existing 10 raw + 10 ware capacities. All movements use
moveGoods(from,to,key,n,reason); named createGoods/destroyGoods cover recipes,
harvest, sales, forge materials, item purchases, renovation and contracts.
Preserve exact goods on demolition and save/load, including fractional stock.
Keep G.v===1. Claims, dispatcher jobs and the <=200-event optional ring live
in RT, not in saves. Claim source quantities and destination space before
walking and release/expire on arrival, cancellation or removal of a carrier.

Porters and production
Porter House / 荷運び小屋: 2x2 and two manually selected v.job slots.
assignJobs preserves assignments but never auto-fills them; chooseSite does
not send porters into production. A bounded dispatcher runs every 0.75 game
seconds. Prioritize low shelves, workshop inputs, output piles and useful
return loads. Score cheap distances and cached connectivity; roadPath only
for the selected trip, never pathLen inside jobs x sites loops.
Existing producer/artisan hauling remains sufficient with zero porters.
Porter carts carry larger loads, freeing producers to work; no rate nerfs.
When a confirmed porter trip blocks work, workers take seated breaks at the
site (4 energy/second, no wound healing). Homes retain 16+/second recovery.
This uses waiting time without changing output rates or production costs.
Pause an unfinished batch visibly when its pile/output buffer is full rather
than silently losing output. Draw small crate/shortage/stockout overlays in
the existing building pass; reuse existing tooltip/long-press detail.

Migration and early income
Merge old shop maps without loss. For old pooled saves, seed only a small
one-time shelf lot withdrawn from unreserved central goods; it represents
stock already on the counter and does not create inventory. Preserve
existing local stock, prices, bonuses and capacity. Workers can top up low
shelves so porters stay optional.

Diagnostics and validation
Before behavior changes: ship ?perf=1 / date 2-second long-press HUD and an
isolated ?simtest=1 runner. Use the game's own weak canvas registry; report
120-frame fps/p95, canvas MB, redraws, cache/collection sizes and save size.
Bound open-ended sprite caches, free evicted canvas backing stores and use
pre-rendered cargo/carts. Trial mobile DPR 2 only if default-zoom art matches.
Run 52 weeks x5 seeded paired scenarios for starter, small, mature +/- two
porters (same and +2 headcount), early duplicate stalls, late, expanded late,
late duplicate shops, demolition and in-memory save/load mid-transit.
Chunk through setTimeout; restore G, RT, VIEW, UI, RNG, dialogs, sound and save
behavior on success, cancellation or error. Record the untouched economy's
baseline before P0 and compare against it. Report conservation, negatives,
reserves, worker/porter time, income, stockouts, cache memory and sim timing.
Native iPhone app-switch survival and Low Power Mode still require on-device
validation; ship the same diagnostics for that purpose. Stop after P0.
