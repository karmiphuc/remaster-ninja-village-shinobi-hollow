SHINOBI HOLLOW — SPECIALIST ARSENAL V19

Audit and scope
The starting game contained 105 pieces of equipment: 32 weapons, 19 armor,
20 headgear, 17 specials and 17 mounts/companions (doctrines excluded).
This update adds 11 pieces, bringing equipment to 116. Existing items remain.
The supplied Ninja Village list informed identities; numeric bonuses were
adapted to Shinobi Hollow's much smaller stat scale.

New equipment

T2 Ninja Claws — ATK +6, DEF +5, Duel +1.
  Short reach (10 versus a katana's 17). Two quick, individually resolved
  duel strikes and a chance of a third. Reduced power per hit; each attack
  can hit, miss, crit or KO independently. Iron claws also reduce an enemy
  melee attack's hit chance by five percentage points. Low raw attack and
  reach offset the extra pressure. Personal infantry equipment.

T2 Ninjato — ATK +9, DEF +1, Duel +2.
  Reach 15. Duel accuracy +6 percentage points, reduced armor mitigation,
  and +20% counter damage. Personal field blows +8%. A katana remains
  cheaper, has more raw ATK for leadership, and arms ordinary troops.

T4 Zanbato — ATK +21, STR +2.
  Broad, heavy anti-cavalry blade. Personal damage +40% versus mounted
  enemies; -8% versus ordinary opponents. Lower duel accuracy, longer
  wind-up and fewer follow-ups. Nodachi retains its own Duel +3 identity.
  Personal infantry equipment.

T3 Two-shot Repeater — MRK +14.
  Two separately resolved shots at 68% power each, then one reload turn.
  In field combat its owner fires on gun phases 1, 3, 5, etc.

T4 Three-shot Rifle — MRK +18.
  Three separately resolved shots at 60% power each, then two reload turns.
  In field combat its owner fires on gun phases 1, 4, 7, etc.
  Both burst guns trade sustained fire for an opening burst. Ordinary
  matchlocks, Thunder Cannon and Kunitomo remain distinct choices.

T3 Ninja Mail — DEF +6, HP +8, MRK +7.
T4 Iga Mail — DEF +9, HP +12, MRK +10.
  Fine indigo chainmail and blackened reinforced mail, without large sode.
  These extend the existing light MRK armor line. Great Armor and Tosei
  still give substantially more DEF/HP; endgame plate remains protective.
  The existing troop arsenal can forge the mail after research and uses
  its MRK bonuses for bows/guns as well as its actual defensive values.

T1 Happuri — DEF +2, HP +1.
  Open iron brow-and-cheek protection. Personal melee damage taken -12%,
  including duels. This extra protection does not stop arrows, bullets or
  grenade blasts. No material purchase cost, like other Tier 1 equipment.

T2 Field Rations — HP +4.
  Recover 20% maximum HP once per battle at a round break below 65% HP.
  Also add 4% formation cohesion resilience, capped at 8% from provisions
  and within the existing overall resilience cap. Defeated commanders
  provide no resilience. Rice and vegetables, no Silk, buy the packet.

T3 Pitch Grenade — one throw per equipped infantry commander per battle.
  A ceramic horoku bomb is thrown before the first available melee exchange.
  Its real field damage scales with STR/MRK and respects defenses, tactics,
  siege protection and remaining targets. It does not add a fabricated
  Infantry Power bonus, duplicate casualty damage or grant free duel hits.

T4 Mountain Wolf — ATK +4, DEF +2, HP +10; rare support companion.
  Its wearer's formation gains +10% attack, using the existing diminishing
  companion stacking. Counts as Strategy 2 if the wearer's Strategy is lower;
  it does not add another two levels to an already skilled strategist.
  Wild hunt opponents use ATK 26 and HP 100 before normal hunt scaling.

Existing equivalents extended; duplicate inventory entries excluded

- Fuma Garb uses the existing Shinobi Garb item ID, shinobi. Its DEF +4,
  HP +4 and MRK +4 remain. Existing owned/equipped copies survive saves.
  It is the starting point of Fuma Garb -> Ninja Mail -> Iga Mail.
- The existing Medicine Case supplies the Herb role: its HP +14 and Duel
  +3 remain, with a real 15% maximum-HP recovery once per battle.
- Rations and Medicine Case share one recovery use per commander per
  battle, choosing the stronger equipped recovery. Duplicate packets do
  not stack healing. Recovery cannot revive a KO or replenish common troops.
- No separate Herb inventory entry, duplicate Shinobi/Fuma armor, generic
  rifle-stat reskin, blanket infantry-power grenade or new consumable
  management interface was added.

Acquisition and visual integration

Research follows existing tiers, point gates and prerequisites. Ninja weapons
branch from the sword line; Repeater follows Samurai-zutsu and Rifle follows
Repeater; the light mail branches from Fuma Garb. Existing early-material
rules remain: Tier 1 needs gold only, and Tier 2 uses no Silk.

Swordsmiths carry claws, Ninjato, Zanbato and Happuri. Sakai carries the burst
firearms, mail and grenade; Nanban traders can carry Rifle. Pilgrim peddlers
carry rations and selected ninja gear. Wolves appear rarely through hunts,
Ookami-clan companion spoils and the beast trader; they are never researched.

Enemy captains can use specialist weapons at the matching tiers, and those
worn weapons can become normal battle spoils. Ordinary troop weapon-forging
priorities stay unchanged; specialist weapons are personal choices. Existing
utility loot pools also include rations and grenades.

Shared procedural modules draw the claws, wide blade, multiple gun barrels,
iron face guard, mail, packets and wolf. Icons, portraits, village citizens,
battle poses, reloads, duel finishers and the Atelier collection use them.
The PNG review sheet is rendered from the actual game code.

Validation
- 1,100 sampled 1v1–3v3 duels: individual HP accounting, real claw combos,
  gun shot/reload cadence, anti-cavalry effects and post-KO action checks.
- Live 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 choreography, talisman and retainer rescues; duplicate
  strike events did not repeat damage. Existing execution timing retained.
- Actual field battle through all four formation phases, grenade impact,
  battle aftermath and elimination. Rifle additionally tested through four
  gun phases: 3 shots, reload, reload, 3 shots; no hidden reload intercepts.
- Both healing rates, shared-use limit, KO exclusion, independent-hero HP
  synchronization, no free recruits, capped resilience and save/load.
- All 11 additions rendered through 99 battle-pose samples, 44 village
  direction samples and portraits; Atelier mixing and 430px mobile layout.
- Existing roads, delivered shop stock, rebellion guarantees, casualty routs,
  finishBattle, duelLoss, hero rescues and save functions preserved.

Limits
Field armies retain the existing aggregate damage model; visual burst bullets
represent that phase's already-resolved damage. Duel bullets and claw strikes
are individually resolved HP attacks. Medicine/rations act at round breaks,
not during an execution or as a new rescue mechanic. Reloads are deliberate
vulnerable turns, so burst weapons do not replace reliable single-shot guns.
